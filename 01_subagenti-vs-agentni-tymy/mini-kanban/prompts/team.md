Mode: AGENT TEAM. Create an agent team and act as its lead. Do not just spawn independent subagents.

Teammates:
1. architect - project structure, Vite/TS config, ARCHITECTURE.md
2. domain-dev - `src/domain/` and `src/store/` plus unit tests for them
3. ui-dev - `src/components/` and the app shell
4. tester - end-to-end check that the app really runs and the flows work

Use the shared task list (with dependencies) and let teammates message each other directly when they
need to agree on the interface. Give every teammate the full interface contract. Wait until all
teammates are finished, make sure the result works, then shut the team down and finish.
